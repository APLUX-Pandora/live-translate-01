Live Translate 01

  1. Press Start to play the built-in lecture with English and Chinese subtitles.
  2. To use your own file, select its language and upload a video with sound or an MP3, WAV or M4A (AAC) audio file. Choose a target language and press Start. Use the media list to switch files.
  3. Press Stop to finish captured audio and pending translations, or Cancel to discard unfinished work and start a new session.
  4. If the connection drops, keep the page open. Retry connection resumes the same session and resends unacknowledged audio.

The device runs the models, and this page captures the selected file’s audio. Seeking during transcription starts a new recording from the new position. If less than one second remains, playback and transcription restart from the beginning. Switching files cancels the current recording and clears its subtitles.
